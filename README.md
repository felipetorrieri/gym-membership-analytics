# 🏋️ Gym Membership Analytics

> **End-to-end Business Intelligence project focused on membership growth, recurring revenue, churn, retention and gym utilization across a multi-location fitness network.**

## 🎯 Overview

This project analyzes a fictional multi-location gym network from three complementary perspectives: **financial performance, customer retention and operational usage**.

The core business journey is:

**Acquisition → Membership → Engagement → Retention → Revenue**

All records, locations, customers and financial values are fully synthetic and were created specifically for public portfolio use.

---

## 📊 Analytical Areas

### 💰 Executive & Revenue Performance
Active Members · MRR · Average Ticket · New Memberships · Cancellations · Delinquency · Net Growth

### 🔄 Retention & Churn
Cancellation Rate · Retention by Plan · Retention by Unit · Average Tenure · Acquisition Quality · Cohort Analysis

### 🏃 Engagement & Operations
Visit Frequency · Peak Hours · Unit Demand · Activity Popularity · Visits per Member · Capacity Utilization

---

## 💼 Business Questions

- Is the active member base growing?
- How is monthly recurring revenue evolving?
- Which units generate the most revenue?
- Which plans have the strongest retention?
- Where is cancellation highest?
- Which acquisition channels bring longer-lasting members?
- How long does the average member remain subscribed?
- Which locations and time slots experience the highest demand?
- Which activities generate the most engagement?
- How does customer engagement relate to retention?

## 🗂️ Data Model

The project combines four synthetic datasets:

- **Members** — member profile, plan, unit, acquisition and cancellation
- **Monthly Membership** — active-base snapshots, MRR and delinquency
- **Visits** — check-ins, activities and time slots
- **Unit Capacity** — operational capacity by location and time slot
